Download Maven Fuzzy Factory from the Maven Analytics Data Playground
(mavenanalytics.io/data-playground), unzip the CSVs into this folder.

Files expected here (they are git-ignored, so they are not part of this repository):
  website_sessions.csv
  website_pageviews.csv
  orders.csv
  order_items.csv
  order_item_refunds.csv
  products.csv
  maven_fuzzy_factory_data_dictionary.csv   (optional; a copy is already in docs/)
